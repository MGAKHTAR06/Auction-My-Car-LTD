About

Built by someone who buys
and sells cars for real.

Auction My Car started from the salvage-and-repair world — buying damaged cars at trade auctions, fixing them, selling them on. That world works because auctions are honest about what a car is: the category is on the listing, the price is decided in the open, and everyone knows the rules before the hammer falls.

Private sellers have never had that. Selling a car privately means strangers from a classifieds site, haggling on the driveway, and hoping a bank transfer is real. So we took the auction format and rebuilt it for ordinary people: every member ID-verified, every listing checked by a real person before it goes live, every payment held until the car and the paperwork have changed hands, and a fixed set of rules — published in plain English — that both sides agree to before a single bid is placed.

We never own the cars and never touch the money on its way between buyer and seller. We run the room: the Saturday auction, the checks, and the adjudication when something needs a human decision. That's the whole business, and we think it's enough.
