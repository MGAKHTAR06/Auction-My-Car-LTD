⚑ Report a problem

For collection issues — a seller who won't answer or won't commit to a time. The seller is notified when you file this, and an admin will contact them. Their £5/day clock keeps running unless they have a valid reason (an admin decides).

📎 Attach screenshots of texts / call log

Raise a dispute — not as described

Covered: damage or missing parts that existed at listing and weren't in the seller's damage declaration. Not covered: faults developing after collection — that's auction risk. Filing freezes all payment clocks and any payout until an admin rules.

📷 Photos and video of the issue (required)

An admin will email the seller for their side and speak to you both by recorded call before ruling in writing. If your dispute is rejected, exiting the purchase costs £100 (paid to the seller) on top of the £50 hold.
