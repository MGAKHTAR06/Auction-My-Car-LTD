3. Bids are binding

Before bidding in any auction you must confirm (a) acceptance of these Terms and (b) that you understand all bids are legally binding. Both confirmations are recorded with a timestamp. A winning bid is a commitment to purchase.

4. Fees

Listing £25 (non-refundable — it funds listing review). Featured slot £10. Verification £5. Buyer's auction fee per the published fee table. Card processing costs are passed through at checkout. VAT is added to our fees only if/when we are VAT-registered.

5. Deposits, late payment and withdrawal

Entering an auction places a £50 card authorisation. Winners must pay within 72 hours; late payment captures the deposit (£25 seller / £25 platform) as compensation and costs. Withdrawing from a winning bid costs a further £100 (£40 seller / £60 platform). After a rejected dispute, withdrawal costs £100 (wholly to the seller) with the £50 retained by the platform for case-review costs.

6. Handover, collection and fund release

Sale funds are held and released to the seller only after evidence checks (New Keeper Slip, two vehicle videos, V5C proof of posting) pass review. Sellers must agree collection within 72 hours of contact release; thereafter £5/day accrues, capped at £70 at 14 days, waived entirely for admin-verified unforeseeable reasons. If the sale collapses through seller inaction, the buyer is refunded in full and the seller bears the fine plus the processing cost of the refund.

7. Disputes

Only undeclared damage existing at listing may be disputed, and only before the buyer confirms receipt. Faults arising after collection are the buyer's risk. Rulings are made in writing after both parties are heard; calls are recorded with notice.
